# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First public release.
- Signs for death (WASTED), death by a guard or a player (BUSTED), the graveyard line, mission passed, mission failed and level up.
- Gray screen on death, under the windows of the game.
- Quips: captions with well-known lines at sixteen moments of the game, each with its switch and your own lines, said in a speech balloon over your character or as a caption.
- Original sounds included; your own files in the Sounds folder play in their place.
- The signs have a big bold font of their own (Bowlby One, SIL Open Font License); a font of yours in the Fonts folder takes its place.
- Each kind of sign can be dragged to where you want it on the screen.
- A window to switch each sign, change its text, try it, and choose the language.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese.
