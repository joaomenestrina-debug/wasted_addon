# Korum Wasted

**Your character knows all the memes.**

Big cinematic signs for the moments of your character, in the style of open-world action games, and a character that
answers the game with the memes everybody knows.

- **Death** - the world goes gray, a dark band crosses the screen and a big red sign drops in: **WASTED**.
- **Busted** - killed by a town guard or by an enemy player? The sign is blue: **BUSTED**.
- **Graveyard** - when you release the spirit, a caption says what you are thinking: *"Ah shit, here we go again."*
- **Mission passed** - hand in a quest and get the golden **MISSION PASSED!** with **RESPECT +** under it.
- **Mission failed** - abandon a quest, or fail one, and the red **MISSION FAILED!** shows with the quest's name.
- **Level up** - **RESPECT +** and your new level.
- **Quips** - your character says well-known lines at sixteen moments, in a speech balloon over its head: low health
  (*"This is fine."*), three deaths in a row, a rare item (*"Stonks."*), a dungeon, the hearthstone, going AFK and more.
  Only you see them: nothing is sent to the chat.

Everything has a sound of its own (original ones, made for the addon), and every sign can be switched off.

## The window

Type `/wasted` (or go to Options > AddOns > Korum Wasted).

- **General** - a button to try each sign, where each kind of sign goes on the screen (drag them), a switch for each
  one, sound on or off, and the language.
- **Quips** - balloon or caption, and for each moment a switch, a try button and your own lines.
- **Texts** - write your own words for every sign, and choose how long the death sign stays.
- **Sounds** - play each sound and see where it comes from.

## Your own sounds and font

Put `.ogg` or `.mp3` files in `Interface/AddOns/KorumWasted/Sounds` with the names listed in `Sounds/README.txt`
(`wasted`, `mission_passed`, `respect`, `graveyard`, and optionally `busted` and `mission_failed`) and they play in place
of the addon's. A font named `Fonts/sign.ttf` or `Fonts/sign.otf` becomes the font of the signs. Close and open the
game after adding files. Only use sounds and fonts you have the right to use.

## Details

- English and Brazilian Portuguese (chosen in the window, or by the game's language).
- Nothing in the game is changed: the addon only shows signs and plays sounds.
- The gray screen sits under the windows of the game, so you can still click to release the spirit.
- Saved data: `KorumWastedDB`. Commands: `/wasted`, and `/wasted death | busted | graveyard | mission | failed | level` to try one.
